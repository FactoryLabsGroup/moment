How it works

One duo, three beats.

01Dealt

A friend and a challenge, at a random moment.

Sometime today, Moment picks a friend from your pool and a challenge neither of you has had lately, and tells you both at once. You won't know which friend it is — you both get the same challenge, and the same clock.

02Shoot

Shoot your half. They shoot theirs.

Open the camera before the clock runs out and retake as often as you like. Nobody sees a thing — not even who you're paired with — until both halves are in.

03Unlock

Two halves. One moment.

The moment the second half lands, the duo unlocks for both of you: two photos clicked into one — a memory that belongs to just the two of you. Now you see who it was.

And if one of you doesn't make it in time? Both halves are deleted, and your partner stays anonymous — for good.

Questions

Good to know.

Something else? Visit support

Can I choose who I'm paired with?

No — that's the fun. Moment picks someone from your pool, preferring whoever you were paired with least recently, so it keeps changing.

Can my partner see my photo before they shoot?

No. Your half isn't sent to their phone until both halves are in. They can't even see that it's you.

What if I miss a duo?

Nothing dramatic. The clock runs out, both halves are deleted, your partner stays anonymous, and the next duo comes along later.

How many duos will I get?

Four a day to start, one every hour or two. Change it to anything from one to ten on your profile, and set quiet hours for when you don't want any.

Do my friends need the same phone as me?

No. iPhone and Android share one Moment, so you can get duos with anyone.

What happens when I change phones?

Connect Google — or Apple, on iPhone — in Settings → Connections. On the new phone, tap “I already have an account” and you're back, with every duo.

Who can see my duos?

Only you and the friend you made each one with. There's no feed and no public profile — nothing for anyone to browse.

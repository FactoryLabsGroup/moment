Privacy policy

Your photos are for two people

Effective 3 October 2026

Moment is an app for iPhone and Android made by Factory Labs (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what Moment collects, why, who helps us run it, how long things are kept, and what you can do about it

In short

  • A duo is seen only by the two people in it. Your partner's half is never sent to your phone, and yours never to theirs, until both halves are in
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs. We never sell your information
  • Photos are stripped of location and camera data and encrypted on our server
  • If a duo's clock runs out, its photos are deleted. You can delete any duo, or your whole account, from the app

What we don't collect

No email address or phone number is needed to sign up. Moment doesn't read your contacts, doesn't use your location, and doesn't use an advertising ID. Location and camera details (EXIF) are removed from every photo when it arrives, and Moment has no analytics, ad or tracking libraries

How your photos are handled

  • Each half is shrunk on your phone before it is sent, and a challenge's filter is applied there too
  • On our server every photo is encrypted under its own key. Photos are reached only through signed links that expire within hours
  • Your partner's half is never returned before the duo unlocks. Until then, they show as “? ? ? ? ?”, unless they are your only friend, in which case the duo shows their name
  • If the clock runs out first, both halves are deleted from our server
  • A finished duo belongs to both of you. Either of you can delete it, for both, at any time; its photos are deleted with it

Who helps us run Moment

We don't share your information with anyone else, and we never sell it

  • Hetzner Online GmbH rents us the servers and the backup storage that Moment runs on. Your data is stored there, under our control
  • Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver notifications to your phone. A notification carries a short line (for example, the challenge) and nothing else
  • Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google, only if you connect them
  • An email provider sends our replies, only if you wrote to us and connected an account with an email address

Some of these providers may process information outside your country, under their own terms and safeguards

How long things are kept

  • Your account and finished duos: until you delete them, or your account
  • A duo that runs out: its photos are deleted when the clock runs out; the empty record goes after 30 days
  • Notification records: 30 days
  • Backups: deleted photos leave our backups the following night. Encrypted database backups roll over within about five weeks
  • Reports: until they are resolved and no longer needed, or until you delete your account

Your choices and rights

  • Change your username, photo, colour and settings on your profile
  • Keep a copy of any duo by saving it to your photos
  • Delete a duo from its own screen, or your whole account from your profile. Deleting your account removes your account, your duos and every photo in them, for good, including your friends' copies of your duos
  • Disconnect Apple or Google in Settings → Connections
  • Turn off notifications in your phone's settings, or set quiet hours in the app

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to us and we'll help. We process your information to provide the service you asked for, and to keep it secure and free of abuse

Children

Moment is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child is using Moment, contact us and we will delete the account

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app. If the English and Georgian versions differ, the English version applies
